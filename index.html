Section 01 — The moment

A planting movement at a critical moment.

0%

of Australians not connected to a local church

0

new ACC churches to be planted by 2032

The ACC is Australia's largest Pentecostal denomination and its most prolific church planting movement. Research from Exponential Australia in 2025 names the number one impediment to planting more churches as simply this: the lack of potential church planters. Not funding. Not vision. Not strategy. People.

There is no accredited pathway that seeds a pioneering culture across the whole church, not just in the individual who eventually puts their hand up. The result is that churches are doing two things simultaneously: pastoring their existing community faithfully, and hoping that the next planter will emerge from somewhere — without creating the conditions for that emergence.

Section 02 — The gap

What the movement has been missing.

The ACC has excellent tools for people who have already decided to plant.

What it hasn't had is an accredited, practitioner-led pathway that works upstream — forming planting instinct in emerging leaders who are still serving faithfully in their sending church and haven't yet named what they sense.

    Lecture topicsBiblical foundations of church multiplication · Limitations of addition-based leadership · Applying multiplication to team building · Building environments that release new church expressions

Planting philosophy & strategy

  • Church Planting - PhilosophyA theology of the Church and the conviction to plant one.
    Lecture topicsThe Bride and the Body · Nourishing the Church · Building through the gifts of the church · Faith to plant beyond the ordinary
  • Christian Living FundamentalsGrace and identity as the foundation for healthy leadership.
    Lecture topicsBiblical foundations of grace and the finished work of Christ · Grace vs. performance · Identity and how it shapes leadership · Building sustainable, grace-centred ministry
4

Practical ministry

  • Professional Practice (100-hr placement)Hands-on ministry under an experienced pastor.
    What it involvesWorking alongside an experienced mentor, you'll complete a 100-hour placement in a format matched to your calling — for example, 8 weekly hours in a local church's youth department. Includes mentoring & discipling, and managing relationships.
  • Elective (Kids, Worship, or Youth Ministry)A specialisation matched to the student's calling.
    Options
    • Kids Ministry — curriculum development, team recruitment, discipleship.
    • Worship Ministry — production & tech, service planning, launch strategy, biblical foundations of worship.
    • Youth Ministry — pastoral care in a youth context, developing leadership teams, running a youth ministry program.

Section 04 — Culture change

Not just what it does to a student. What it does to a church.

A church that hosts the Diploma does not simply graduate one or two trained planters. Something happens to the whole community. Multiplication conversations appear across the church. The language of pioneering shows up organically in different places. Volunteers who have never considered planting begin asking questions they hadn't known to ask.

  1. 1

    Pipeline clarity

    Leaders who sense a call now have a defined pathway without leaving the church they love.

  2. 2

    Cultural language

    The vocabulary of multiplication becomes part of how the church tells its story — and its future.

  3. 3

    Apostolic confidence

    The sending church grows in conviction that planting is the call on this church, in this season.

Section 05 — How it works

Inside the Local Church College.

The Diploma of Church Planting Leadership is delivered through Alphacrucis University College's Local Church College framework — the same model already powering diplomas in ministry, leadership, and business across the ACC.

A hosting church provides space and a local LCC coordinator. Alphacrucis handles accreditation, enrolment, and academic oversight — exactly as it does for every other LCC award.
